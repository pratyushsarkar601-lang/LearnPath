# LearnPath

**Learn smarter. Track every topic.**

A personalized adaptive learning and curriculum-tracking platform that helps students explore any board's syllabus topic-by-topic, learn with an AI tutor, test themselves with quizzes, and track their own progress — while bridging the rural–urban education gap.

## About

In one classroom, students learn at very different speeds — yet everyone gets the same content, same pace, and the same quiz. LearnPath solves this by giving every student their own path: browse the real curriculum for their board and class, get topic-level explanations and an AI tutor on demand, take quizzes to find weak spots, and track progress over time.

---

## How It Works

### 1. Role & Class Setup
On first visit, the user picks their role — **Student** or **Teacher** — and selects their **Board** (CBSE, ICSE/ISC, or WBBSE/WBCHSE), **Class** (1–12), and **Section**. They then sign in with Google or email/password.

### 2. Dashboard
The central hub showing the full scale of the curriculum — **3 boards, 20 classes, 113 subjects, 701 chapters, 1685+ topics** — plus personal learning statistics: topics completed, chapters completed, study time, quiz average, and current streak. Includes "Continue Learning" and "Subject Progress" shortcuts.

### 3. Curriculum Browser
Board → Class → Subject → Chapter → Topic navigation. Each subject (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Computer Science, English, Commerce subjects, etc.) shows its chapter list with live progress bars.

### 4. Topic Page
Every topic includes:
- **What You Will Learn** — core ideas and definitions
- **Concept Explanation** — detailed, class-appropriate explanation
- **Important Formulae** — key formulas for the topic
- **Examples & Practice** — worked examples and practice questions
- **AI Tutor** — a chat box where students can ask anything about the topic and get instant, tailored explanations

### 5. Quizzes
Each topic has a short quiz. Answers are checked instantly (correct in green, incorrect in red), and a final score is shown right away — helping students immediately see which concepts need more work.

### 6. Search
A global search bar lets students find any topic across all boards, classes, and subjects instantly, instead of navigating the full hierarchy.

### 7. Resource Library
Teachers can share supplementary study materials (notes, PDFs, etc.), filterable by board, class, and subject, for students to browse.

### 8. Activity Record
A full personal timeline — topics completed, chapters completed, study time, quiz average, current streak, longest streak, quizzes taken — plus a chronological log of every topic started, viewed, or completed.

### 9. Account Settings
Students can view their profile, and update preferred board, class, and section anytime. Notification preferences (progress updates, quiz results, study reminders) are also managed here.
